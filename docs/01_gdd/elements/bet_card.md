Bet cards represents the bets that the player can place on a bot.
The [bet_value](#bet_value) can differ from the [bet_cost](#bet_cost).
# bet_value
It is the amount of money that is assigned to the [placed_bet](placed_bet.md) when [placing a bet](place_bet).
# bet_cost
The amount subtracted from the player's money when they [place a bet](place_bet.md).
# bet_effect
Bet cards can have special effects that can be triggered at different stages of the process:
- when the bet is placed
- when the bot fight resolves
