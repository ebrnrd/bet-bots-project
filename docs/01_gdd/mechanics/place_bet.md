Bets are placed by dragging a [[bet_card]] on a [[bot]] in the [[betting_scene]].
Placing a bet consumes player's money equal to the cost of the [[bet_card]].
The player can place a bet only if they have more or equal money than the cost of the [[bet_card]].
When a bet is placed a [[placed_bet]] is created and assigned to the selected [[bot]].
The value of the [[placed_bet]] is equal to the bet value of the [[bet_card]].
The odds of the [[placed_bet]] are set to the odds of the [[bot]] at the moment of placing the bet.
