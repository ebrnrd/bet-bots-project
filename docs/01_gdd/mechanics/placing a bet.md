Bets are placed by dragging a [[bet card]] on a [[bot]] in the [[betting_scene]].
Placing a bet consumes [[money]] equal to the cost of the [[bet card]].
The player can place a bet only if they have more or equal [[money]] than the cost of the [[bet card]].
When a bet is placed a [[placed bet]] is created and assigned to the selected [[bot]]. The value of the [[placed bet]] is equal to the bet value of the [[bet card]]. The odds of the [[placed bet]] are set to the [[bot odds]] at the moment of placing the bet.
