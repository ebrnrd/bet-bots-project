time is divided in seasons, weeks, days and chunks.
- 1 season contains 4 weeks
- 1 week contains 7 days
- 1 day contains 6 chunks
The player can use a button to [progress_time](progress_time.md). When the player does it on the last time chunk of the day, the day progresses to the next one. The same is true for days to weeks and weeks to seasons.
# Time Chunk
Time chunks are the slots of time that can contain events that can happen during the day, like bot fights of life events.
